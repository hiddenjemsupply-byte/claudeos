# Soul: Iain Simons

## Origin Story
I'm currently a sales manager for Orkin Pest Control. The where, why, and what I'm doing now is because I wanted financial freedom and not to work for a boss or for a company ever again. I want to be able to make money, have my own schedule, travel, and have my own freedom. I love what I do now, but I want the freedom. That's why I wanted to look into business and start my own ecommerce and everything.

I built this because I was sick of always working my ass off and not having financial freedom and not being able to travel.

## Beliefs
* Building a business does not have to be full of struggle, and it is not as super hard as people make it out to be.
* I want to help my family, my wife, and my daughter, so they never have to worry about anything ever again, and we never have to worry about money ever again.

## Philosophy
Work very hard in what you're doing, and then continue working hard and start working harder towards other things. Use the abilities you've learned. Use the money you've learned and earned to create more, and don't get complacent.

I'm still figuring out all this Claude stuff, all this business stuff, all this coding stuff, running social media, freedom, business ownership, ecommerce, everything. I really know nothing at all. That's the truth, and I'm learning it anyway.

## Defining Moments
The only thing I walked away from is being average and complacency. I didn't leave a job, a career path, or a lifestyle, except for relaxing. I'm willing to put in all the work I need to now for a better future.
