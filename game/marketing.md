# Jemfall: Marketing Plan v0.1

Handles: **@playjemfall** on every platform
Public email: hello@jemfall.com
Plugins: Iain sets these up himself

## The Rule
Build a piece of the game, then market that piece. Every build phase ends with a marketing push before the next phase starts. Building is the content.

## The Angle
Nothing personal. No face, no job, no family. The game is the star, and **the Guide is the face of Jemfall**: the in game Guide character talks to the audience through a pixel text box. Clips are pixel art, screen recordings, AI video, and on screen text. The Guide's voice is Iain's own recording run through a voice changer. Tool stack: game/phase0-launch.md, section Tools.

## What Each Platform Does
* **TikTok, YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels:** discovery. 30 to 60 second vertical clips, edited for short form, not cut down from long videos. Practitioner reports say straight reposts of long videos do worse than clips made for short form.
* **YouTube (long):** the main devlog. Deeper updates every couple of weeks.
* **Reddit:** feedback and early players. r/incremental_games is the core audience (Melvor and idle game players). Developers there post playable prototypes and ask for feedback. Read each subreddit's rules before posting, every time.
* **Discord:** home base. Where fans vote on features, playtest, and talk to each other. Servers that only post announcements tend to die, so it needs real conversation.
* **X:** screenshots, quick progress posts, talking with other game devs.

## Content Pillars
1. **Build progress:** "here's what I built this week"
2. **Player decisions:** ask the audience to vote on real choices (class names, boss ideas, biome order). People who help design it stick around.
3. **The Guide:** the Guide answers one player question per clip, in character
4. **Reveals:** classes, bosses, Jems, dimensions, each one gets its own post

## One Ask Per Post
Every post asks for one thing, not a stack.
* Now through Phase 3: **join the Discord**
* Phase 4 on: **play Jemfall**

## Marketing Beat for Each Build Phase
Phase 0 kit (script, captions, Discord setup, first two weeks): game/phase0-launch.md

0. **Plan:** teaser (a Jem falls from the sky), Jem reveal, a few Guide clips. Open the Discord. Keep it short and move to Phase 1.
1. **Solo prototype:** first clip of stepping across the map. Discord members become the first testers.
2. **Core loop:** show crafting chains, the first fight, the first boss. Discord playtests.
3. **Shared world:** first time real players are in the world together. Big moment, big video.
4. **Public alpha:** launch posts on Reddit (r/incremental_games first) and every platform. Ask: play Jemfall.
5 and on: every new dimension, boss, and season gets its own launch push. Season launches every 3 months are built in content.

## Starting Rhythm (adjust once we see what works)
* 3 to 5 short clips a week
* 1 long YouTube devlog every 2 weeks
* 1 Discord update a week, plus a vote or question
* Reddit only when there's something real to show or ask

## Track Every Week
* Discord members
* Followers on each platform
* Views on the best and worst clip, and why
* Playtesters (once there's something to play)
