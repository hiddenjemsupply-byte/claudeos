# Jemfall: Phase 0 Launch Kit v2

Goal: announce Jemfall, open the Discord, and get the first Day One members. The only ask: **join the Discord.**

## Ground Rules
* Nothing personal. No face, no job, no family. The game is the star.
* **The Guide is the face of Jemfall.** The Guide character from the game talks to the audience through a pixel text box on screen. Every clip builds the character players will meet in the game.
* No camera needed. Clips are pixel art, screen recordings, and on screen text with music.
* No AI voices. Same backlash risk as AI art. Text on screen, or your own voice later if you ever want to.

## Why This Works
* Faceless clips are normal for games. Indie Game Joe built a following on short gameplay clips with short text and has driven tens of thousands of Steam wishlists for indie games.
* Polish matters less than the moment. A 15 second screen recording with text can beat a heavily edited long video.
* A developer whose TikTok took a game from 400 to 20,000 wishlists said his next move would be making a game character the face of every video. That's the Guide.

## The Catch
Faceless content needs something to show, and right now there's only art. So Phase 0 stays short: one teaser, a few Guide clips, open the Discord, then move fast into Phase 1. The moment you can step across a map, there's endless footage.

## AI Disclosure
It's out of the hook now, but don't hide it. Put it in a Discord FAQ: "The code is written with AI help. The art is made by hand." Answer straight if anyone asks.

## 2. Guide Clips (the weekly format)
One question per clip, 10 to 20 seconds. Pixel text box types out the Guide's answer over a game visual.

* **"Is there a max level?"** Guide: *Nope. Your skills keep going forever. I've seen someone chop trees for a week straight. I worry about them.*
* **"What are Jems?"** Guide: *The shiny pink things that fell from the sky. You can buy outfits with them. You can't buy power. I checked.*
* **"Why are some places so dark?"** Guide: *The further you get from home, the darker it gets. That's not a metaphor. Bring a torch.*
* **"Is this hard to get into?"** Guide: *That's what I'm for. Ask me anything. No wiki needed.*
* **"What's a season?"** Guide: *Every 3 months, everybody starts fresh and races to the top. You keep your cosmetics. You lose your excuses.*

## 4. Discord Setup
Discord's own guide says: turn on Community, keep the server about one game, start lean, and add channels later.

**Settings**
* Server Settings, Enable Community. This turns on onboarding, announcement channels, and lets people find the server.
* Server icon: game/art/jemfall-avatar.png
* Verification level: Medium (account must be 5 minutes old). Cuts down on spam bots.
* Turn on AutoMod for spam and harmful links.

**Channels (start with only these)**
* START HERE
  * #welcome (read only)
  * #rules (read only)
  * #faq (read only)
  * #announcements (read only, set as an announcement channel)
* JEMFALL
  * #general
  * #ideas
  * #votes
  * #devlog

Add #playtest and #bugs when Phase 1 has something playable. Empty channels make a server feel dead.

**Roles**
* **Dev:** you
* **Day One:** everyone who joins before the first playable version. Give it through an onboarding question (example: "What brought you to Jemfall?" where every answer gives Day One). Turn it off the day Phase 1 ships, so it stays rare.
* **Playtester:** added in Phase 1

**Day One promise (needs your OK):** Day One members get a Day One title in the game. It costs nothing to build and gives people a reason to join now. Only promise it if you'll keep it.
