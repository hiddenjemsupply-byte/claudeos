# Jemfall: Phase 0 Launch Kit

Goal: announce Jemfall, open the Discord, and get the first Day One members. The only ask: **join the Discord.**

## Two Calls for Iain Before Filming
1. **Say you're using AI to help write the code.** Recommendation: yes, say it up front. It's true, and "never lie" is your rule. Gamers in 2026 go after games they think are hiding AI, and getting caught later is far worse than saying it on day one. The art stays hand made, which is where the anger actually is.
2. **Don't name your employer.** "Sales manager" tells the story. Naming the company in public content can cause trouble at your current job.

## 1. Announcement Video (TikTok, Reels, Shorts)
Under 60 seconds. Straight to camera. No intro, no "hey guys." The first line is the hook, and research says you get about 1.5 seconds before people scroll.

**Script**
> (Jem on screen behind you)
> I'm a sales manager. I've never coded anything in my life. And I'm building an MMO.
>
> It's called Jemfall.
>
> I'm doing this for my wife and my daughter. I want to build something that's mine, so we never have to worry about money ever again.
>
> If you grew up on Old School RuneScape, or you've put way too many hours into Melvor Idle, this is for you. You click to step across the map. You chop, mine, fish, and craft your own gear. You fight a lot of bosses. Your skills never stop leveling, and it keeps going while you're offline. It runs right in your browser, on your phone or your PC.
>
> Here's the honest part. I know nothing about this. I'm learning to code with AI helping me, and I'm going to show you every step. The wins and the screwups.
>
> I want you building it with me. The Discord is in my bio. Join now and you're Day One.

About 140 words, around 55 seconds at a normal pace. Cut the Melvor line if it runs long.

## 3. Discord Setup
Discord's own guide says: turn on Community, keep the server about one game, start lean, and add channels later. Removing things is harder than adding them.

**Settings**
* Server Settings, Enable Community. This turns on onboarding, announcement channels, and lets people find the server.
* Server icon: game/art/jemfall-avatar.png
* Verification level: Medium (account must be 5 minutes old). Cuts down on spam bots.
* Turn on AutoMod for spam and harmful links.

**Channels (start with only these)**
* START HERE
  * #welcome (read only)
  * #rules (read only)
  * #announcements (read only, set as an announcement channel)
* JEMFALL
  * #general
  * #ideas
  * #votes
  * #devlog

Add #playtest and #bugs when Phase 1 has something playable. Not before. Empty channels make a server feel dead.

**Roles**
* **Founder:** you
* **Day One:** everyone who joins before the first playable version. Give it through an onboarding question (example: "What brought you to Jemfall?" where every answer gives Day One). Turn it off the day Phase 1 ships, so it stays rare.
* **Playtester:** added in Phase 1

**Day One promise (needs your OK):** Day One members get a Day One title in the game. It costs nothing to build, it gives people a real reason to join now, and players remember who kept their word. Only promise it if you'll keep it.

**#welcome message**
> Welcome to Jemfall.
>
> I'm Iain. I'm a sales manager with zero coding experience, and I'm building an MMO in public. Skilling like Old School RuneScape and Melvor Idle, gear and bosses like Diablo, and it all runs in your browser.
>
> You're early. Everyone who joins before the first playable version is Day One, and Day One gets a title in the game.
>
> Read #rules, say hi in #general, and drop anything you want in the game in #ideas. I read all of it.

**#rules message**
> 1. Be decent. No hate, harassment, or slurs.
> 2. No spam and no promoting other stuff.
> 3. Keep it on topic in each channel.
> 4. Disagree all you want, just keep it about ideas, not people.
> 5. Mods have the final say.

**First #announcements post**
> Jemfall is official.
>
> Here's what it is: click to step across a pixel art map, level skills forever, craft your own gear, fight a lot of bosses, and keep progressing while you're offline. Three classes, seasons every 3 months, and Jems that never buy power.
>
> Right now it's a plan and some art. I'm posting every step of the build in #devlog. Vote in #votes, because you're helping design this.

**First #votes poll (use Discord's poll feature)**
> The 3 starting classes have working names: Warrior, Ranger, Mage. Keep them or rename them?
> * Keep them
> * Rename them (drop your names in #ideas)

## 4. First Two Weeks of Posts
3 to 5 short clips a week, matching the marketing plan. Every clip asks people to join the Discord.

* **Day 1:** announcement video on TikTok, Reels, Shorts. X post. Discord opens.
* **Day 2:** Jem reveal. Post the Jem image: "This is a Jem. It's the currency in Jemfall, and it will never buy power." No pay to win hits hard with OSRS players.
* **Day 4:** short: why Jemfall has no max level
* **Day 6:** short: the colors tell you how far from home you are (look test image)
* **Day 8:** short: why Old School and Melvor are so hard to get into, and the Guide character that fixes it in Jemfall
* **Day 10:** short: class name vote results from Discord
* **Day 12:** short: seasons every 3 months, what resets and what you keep
* **Day 14:** YouTube long devlog #0: the whole plan, start to finish, plus the Discord count so far

Reddit stays quiet in Phase 0. Nothing playable yet, so there's nothing real to show r/incremental_games.

## 5. Track at the End of Week 2
* Discord members and Day One count
* Followers on each platform
* Best and worst clip by views, and a guess at why

## Then
Phase 1 build starts: the solo prototype (one biome, click to step, one gathering skill, a bank, offline progress, the Guide). Step one is researching and picking the tech.
