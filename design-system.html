xerox

A visual language of photocopied paper, faded film, heavy lowercase type, and photos cropped into square stair-steps. Quiet captions sit at the edges.

Derived from a reference poster: "nature is beatiful" (sic). Tokens, components, and composition rules below.

color

01

The page is two tones: warm photocopy paper and soft black ink. All color comes from photography. The film palette is for photo tints, placeholders, and the occasional accent. Never use it for type.

type

02

space

03

Most spacing sits on an 8pt grid. Photo crops use a separate step unit, and every notch is 1×, 2×, or 3× step. On a 9:16 poster the step is roughly 2.5% of the width.

--s1 4
--s2 8
--s3 16
--s4 24
--s5 40
--s6 64
--s7 104
--step 28

Composition grid: 12 columns with no gutters. Elements snap to column edges and overlap freely. Headline words hang off the left margin, and captions hug the right edge or sit in the gaps between crops.

stepped crops

04

This is the signature move. Photos never show as plain rectangles. Each corner is notched in square stair-steps, as if pixels or puzzle tiles had been knocked out. Type and caption plates sit inside the notches.

.step-a: notch top-left (2×1), bottom-right (1×2)

.step-b: left-side ladder plus a bottom-left bite

.step-c: top-right shelf, bottom-left 1×1

.step-d: plus / cross, all four corners

<div class="photo step-a" style="--step:24px">
  <img src="meadow.jpg" alt="">
</div>

ornaments

05

Sparkle. A four-point star with concave sides. Use three sizes (34 / 22 / 13) and 3–5 per composition. Put them in empty paper, never on a photo.

Crosshair. Hairline registration marks, 1px ink at 75%. The vertical arm is longer. They hint at print trim marks.

Healing is a slow, deliberate mending of a broken mosaic.

Caption plate. Paper-colored block, 8px padding, overlapping a photo edge. Text aligns flush left, ragged right.

rules

07
Do
  • Keep the scale gap extreme: display at 12–17% of canvas width, captions near 2%
  • Let headline words share space with crops: tuck them into notches
  • Desaturate and warm every photo (sat .72, sepia .12)
  • Apply grain to everything, including type
  • Leave 30–40% of the canvas as bare paper
  • Crop captions mid-sentence; fragments read as found text
Don't
  • Use rounded corners, drop shadows, or gradients on UI
  • Set type in film colors; type is ink only
  • Use uppercase display type
  • Center anything
  • Use mid-size type (18–40px) on posters
  • Show photos as clean rectangles
