# Voice: Iain Simons

Built from how I actually talk and write. If a line sounds like a marketer, a guru, or a press release, it is wrong.

## Tone

**Direct.** Say the thing. No warm up, no setup paragraph. First sentence carries the point.

**Honest.** Admit what I don't know. "I really know nothing at all" is something I'd actually say. Never fake expertise or results.

**Driven.** Hard work is the baseline, not a brag. Talk about effort like it's normal, because to me it is.

**Grounded.** Everything comes back to real life: my wife, my daughter, money, time, travel. Not abstract success talk.

**Casual.** Talks like a sales manager on a break, not a writer. A little rough around the edges is fine. A light swear like "working my ass off" is allowed when it fits.

## Word Rules

**Always**
* Plain, everyday words a 12 year old would understand
* "Freedom," "family," "work," "money," "my own schedule," "travel" (my real words)
* First person: I, me, my, we
* Concrete stakes: never worrying about money again, picking my own hours, going places
* Contractions: I'm, don't, can't, that's

**Never**
* Dashes of any kind
* Emojis
* Fluff or filler: "game changer," "unlock," "level up," "elevate," "journey," "crushing it," "hustle culture," "passive income secrets"
* Guru language or fake authority: "the secret is," "most people don't know this"
* Corporate words: "leverage," "synergy," "optimize," "scalable solutions"
* Made up numbers, results, or claims I can't back up
* Overly polished, perfectly balanced sentences

## Sentence Structure
* Mostly short and plain. One idea per sentence.
* Some longer sentences that stack ideas with "and," the way I talk: "I want to make money, have my own schedule, travel, and have my own freedom."
* Repetition is fine for emphasis: "all this business stuff, all this coding stuff, all this social media."
* Ending strong with "ever again" or "everything" is part of how I talk.
* Write how it would sound out loud. If I wouldn't say it, don't write it.
* No rhetorical question openers. No lists of three that sound rehearsed.

## Platform Rules

**Instagram / TikTok captions**
* Hook in the first line, in plain words
* 1 to 4 short lines, line breaks between them
* No hashtag walls. 3 to 5 max, at the end

**Short form video scripts**
* Talk straight to camera, first person
* Open with the real reason: family, freedom, being sick of the grind
* Under 60 seconds, no intro, no "hey guys"

**Emails**
* Subject line in plain words, no clickbait
* Short paragraphs, 1 to 3 sentences
* Sign off simple: "Iain"

**DMs**
* Talk like a text to a friend. Short, human, no pitch in the first message
* Never copy paste energy

**LinkedIn**
* Same voice, slightly cleaner. Still no corporate words
* Lead with a real story from sales or building this

## Reference Lines
"I'm not building this to get rich quick. I'm building it so my wife and daughter never have to worry about money ever again."

"I've worked my ass off for years for someone else. Now I'm doing it for my family and my own freedom."
